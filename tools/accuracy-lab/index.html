Forecast accuracy — lab

How this is calculated (v1 method)
  • Speed is scored at every wind speed: was the forecast within ±2 m/s of the meter?
  • Direction is scored only above a cutoff (default 3 m/s, on the meter), because below that direction is physically meaningless — a cup anemometer barely turns and the model is guessing. A site with a verified higher minimum wind raises its cutoff.
  • The headline % is the share of launchable hours (at or above the cutoff) within both tolerances. Light-wind hours are still recorded, still counted for speed, and still shown as their own band — they are simply not in the headline denominator.
  • Reference quality: a score is only as good as the station it is measured against. "good" = verified and ≤5 km; "provisional" = unverified, unknown distance, or 5–15 km; "unusable" = no station, >15 km, or the same station attached to two sites.
  • Stability compares the first half of the period with the second — big swings mean the figure is not yet settled.
  • Sample size (n, and distinct days) is shown beside every figure. Below the minimum, a site says "collecting".
  • A score describes forecast versus that site's anemometer, never versus the launch. The meter may sit a kilometre away on a different aspect.

How to read this.
  • Hit% = share of measured hours where the forecast was within the stated speed and direction tolerance.
  • Confident requires enough hours; below that a figure is shown as collecting and must not be trusted.
  • A score describes forecast versus that site's anemometer, not versus the launch — the meter may sit a kilometre away on a different aspect.
  • falseGreen counts hours we showed flyable while the meter read over the site's own limit. It should be zero.
