How to read this.
  • Hit% = share of measured hours where the forecast was within the stated speed and direction tolerance.
  • Confident requires enough hours; below that a figure is shown as collecting and must not be trusted.
  • A score describes forecast versus that site's anemometer, not versus the launch — the meter may sit a kilometre away on a different aspect.
  • falseGreen counts hours we showed flyable while the meter read over the site's own limit. It should be zero.
